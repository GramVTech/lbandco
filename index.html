Our Story

A Legacy of
Tamil Flavours

Founded in the heart of Hosur, Tamil Nadu, Lakshmi Bharathi & Co brings the authentic taste of traditional South Indian cuisine to every plate we serve. Our kitchen is rooted in the rich culinary heritage passed down through generations.

From the aroma of freshly ground masalas to the golden crispness of our dosas, every dish is crafted with love, tradition, and the finest locally sourced ingredients. We believe food is a celebration of culture.

Beyond our restaurant, we extend the warmth of our kitchen to your celebrations — weddings, festivals, corporate events, and community gatherings across Tamil Nadu.

Our Restaurant

Savour the soul of Tamil Nadu in every bite

A Feast of Authentic Tastes

Step into Lakshmi Bharathi & Co and be transported to a traditional Tamil home. Our open kitchen, warm ambience, and the comforting fragrance of South Indian spices welcome you every day.

We serve fresh breakfast, hearty lunch meals, evening tiffin, and dinner. Every meal is cooked fresh to order, using traditional clay pots and stone-ground ingredients.
